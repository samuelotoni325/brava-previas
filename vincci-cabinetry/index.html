How a custom project works

Four steps, one team.

Every step is explained before it happens, so you always know where your project is.

  1. 01

    Consult

    You send photos, measurements or plans, and tell us how you use the room. We listen first.

  2. 02

    Design

    Layout, finishes and storage are drawn for your space, and adjusted with you until it is right.

  3. 03

    Build

    The cabinetry is built to the inch before it ever reaches your home.

  4. 04

    Install

    Delivered and installed, with the room protected while the crew works.

About Vincci

Craftsmanship, dedication and a little innovation.

Vincci Cabinetry was founded in 2019 to bring craftsmanship, dedication and innovation to custom cabinetry and woodworking. The team, led by Alex Coelho, works from Winter Garden, Florida, with more than 20 years of expertise behind it.

The goal is simple: turn your vision into a room, with precision and quality you can see from across it and feel when you open a drawer.

FAQ
Before you pick up the phone.

    
01Do you only build kitchens?
No. Kitchens are a big part of the work, but Vincci also builds closets, living room panels and wall units, home offices, vanities, bars and single pieces of bespoke furniture.
02How much does custom cabinetry cost?
Every project is built to its own measurements, materials and finishes, so there is no price list. The quote is free: send photos or measurements and you get a number for your project, not an average.
03Do you work outside Winter Garden?
Yes. Vincci is based in Winter Garden, offers faster service across Florida and delivers across the United States. Tell us where the project is and we will tell you how it works for your location.
04What should I send to get a quote?
Photos of the space, rough measurements and any inspiration pictures you have saved. Plans or drawings are welcome too, by text or at sales@vinccicabinetry.com.
05Can I see more of your work first?
Of course. The gallery above shows finished projects, and new work goes up on Instagram at @vincciorlando.
06Who will I be talking to?
The Vincci team is led by Alex Coelho, and the number on this page, (689) 222-4967, is Vincci’s own.
