02 · Behind the tile

      
Five layers you’ll never see.

      
A shower that lasts is built under the tile. Scroll to pull the layers apart.

      
  1. Tile, on the laserSet level and spaced evenly, line by line.
  2. Thinset, notchedCombed with a notched trowel for full contact.
  3. Waterproof membraneSeams and corners sealed, so water stays in the shower.
  4. Cement boardA solid backer that won’t rot when it gets wet.
  5. Studs & subfloorThe frame has to be flat before anything else.

09 · Questions

  
Good to know.

  
Do I need to buy the tile myself?
Either way works. On Thumbtack, some clients already had their tile and others asked Jale Floors to buy it. Choose in the estimate.
What materials do you install?
Ceramic, porcelain, marble, travertine, natural stone and mosaic.
Do you waterproof showers?
Yes. Waterproofing is part of shower and upstairs bathroom work, and of repairs when broken tile let water in.
Which areas do you cover?
We’re based in Marietta, GA. Tell us your town in the estimate and we’ll confirm.
How do I pay?
Apple Pay, cash, check, credit card or Zelle.
Who runs the job?
Joaquim Filho, with a team of ten. Ten years in business, background check listed on Thumbtack.
