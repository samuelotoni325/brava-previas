Good questions.

  
01My floor looks ruined. Is it worth saving?
Most solid hardwood can be sanded again. Send a few photos by text and we’ll tell you honestly if it’s a refinish or a replacement.
02Someone else already worked on my floor and it came out bad. Can you fix it?
That’s a job we know. One client wrote that we “saved our floors from a previous contractor who destroyed them.”
03What is the water-drop test?
A rule of thumb: drop a little water on a worn spot. If it beads, the finish still seals. If it soaks in, the finish is gone. We always confirm in person.
04How do I get a price?
Tap the three steps at the top of this page and send the text, with photos if you can. We reply, see the floor and give you a written estimate.
