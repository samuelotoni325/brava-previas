FAQ

Good questions, straight answers.

What does a deep cleaning cover?

It is the total reset: hidden dirt, grime and buildup in every corner of the home, top to bottom. Tell us what bothers you most in the message and we plan around it.

How does the 20% off work?

It is for your first cleaning, as in our Facebook and Instagram ad. Keep “Apply 20% off” checked in the quote and it goes in your message.

How often can you come?

One-time, weekly, bi-weekly or monthly. Regular clients get a fixed day and time.

Do you clean Airbnbs and offices?

Yes. Short-term rentals get fresh linens, kitchen, bathrooms and staging between guests, and small offices get a plan with no paperwork or extra costs.

Can you clean after a renovation?

Yes: paint splatters, grout residue, plaster, cement and the dust that settles everywhere.

Will I know who is coming to my home?

Always. Clear, honest communication is one of our three promises: you know who is coming and what to expect.

Where do you work?

Boca Raton, Deerfield Beach, Pompano Beach and across South Florida. If your city is not on the list, pick “Somewhere else” and type it.
