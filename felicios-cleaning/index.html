FAQ

Before you text.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

Not at all. Most of our clients give us a code or leave a key. We treat your home with total respect.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, we bring everything, including eco-friendly products. Prefer we use yours? Just let us know.

How long does a cleaning take?

It depends on size and condition. A standard 3-bedroom home usually takes 2 to 4 hours.

How is the price set?

By the size of the home, the type of cleaning and the condition of the property. Estimates are free, with no hidden fees, and recurring weekly, bi-weekly and monthly cleanings get a discount.

Can I reschedule?

Absolutely. Just text us at least 24 hours in advance and we’ll find a new time that works.

Are pets okay during the cleaning?

Yes, we love pets. Let us know their names and any special notes when you book.

Do you speak Portuguese?

Yes. We also speak Portuguese.
