02 · A day across the river

      
While you’re in Manhattan, home gets its day too.

      
  1. 8:10
    You head across the river.
    PATH, ferry or the Turnpike. The apartment stays behind, just as you left it.
  2. 10:30
    Kitchen first.
    Counters, sink, stovetop and the fronts of the appliances.
  3. 12:15
    Bathrooms shine.
    Mirror, glass, fixtures, toilet and floor.
  4. 2:40
    Beds made, pillows back.
    The room you see before you sleep, put back together.
  5. 4:20
    Floors, and the lines.
    Vacuumed edge to edge, mopped where it matters.
  6. 6:05
    You open the door.
    That’s the whole idea.
