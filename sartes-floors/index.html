FAQ
Before you book the walkthrough.

    
01Is the estimate really free?
Yes. One hour at the property: we measure every room, check what is under the existing floor and leave you a written number. It costs nothing and commits you to nothing.
02Do I need to go to a showroom?
No. We bring the samples to your house and lay them in the room they are going into, so you choose in your own light.
03Can you turn carpeted stairs into wood?
Yes, it is the job we get called for by name: every riser cut to the actual opening, every tread matched to the floor below.
04Who does the work?
Our own crews, not day labour. The crew that shows up on Tuesday is the crew that shows up on Thursday, with one person to call for the whole job.
05Can I send plans or a material list?
Yes. Plans, drawings and material lists are welcome at estimate@sartesrenovations.com.
06What areas do you cover?
Sixteen cities from West Palm Beach to Miami, all within one drive-time radius. Outside the list? Call anyway: we travel for whole-house work.
