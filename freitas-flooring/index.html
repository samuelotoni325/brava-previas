Under the shine

      
Seven layers. You only see the last one.

      
  1. 01Subfloor
    We check it flat and solid before a single board goes down.
  2. 02Underlayment
    A layer between subfloor and wood, against moisture and squeaks.
  3. 03Tongue & groove
    Each board locks into the last one. No gaps, no lips.
  4. 04Blind nailing
    Nailed through the tongue at an angle, so you never see it.
  5. 05Sanding
    Down to clean, flat, bare wood.
  6. 06Stain (optional)
    Natural, or the color you choose.
  7. 07Finish coats
    Sealed and coated in the sheen you picked.

      
Drawing of the craft, not to scale. Every floor is checked on the visit.

Questions
Asked before every job.

  
Do you install vinyl too, or only hardwood?
Both. Our van reads “Hardwood floor installation” and “Vinyl flooring specialist”, and clients have hired us for hardwood and vinyl in the same job.
My floors look ruined. Can they be saved?
Often, yes. One client wrote: “We didn’t think our floors could be restored but they look as good as new now!” Send a photo on WhatsApp and we’ll tell you honestly.
Can you fix just one damaged area?
Yes. Damaged boards can be cut out and new ones laced in. After sanding and finishing, the patch blends into the floor.
How do I get a price?
Send the three taps at the top of this page on WhatsApp, with a few photos. We set a time to see the floor and give you a written estimate.
Which sheen should I choose?
Matte and satin hide everyday scuffs best. Semi-gloss and gloss show off the wood and reflect the light. Try the dial above and add your pick to the request.
