05 · Know what you’re booking
House cleaner vs. housekeeper
They are different services.

  

    
Our service
A house cleaner
  • Focuses on cleaning and hygienizing your home.
  • Keeps your property clean, organized and fresh.
  • Works based on the agreed time and services.

    vs
    
Different service
A housekeeper
  • Performs household management tasks on a regular basis.
  • May include laundry, dishes, organizing personal belongings, inside cabinets, and other daily household responsibilities.
  • Usually works more hours and has a different type of contract.

08 · FAQ
Good to know.

  
How do I get a price?
Answer the three questions at the top and text them to Kelly. Every home is different, so the estimate comes from her, based on your space and the services you pick.
What’s included in a regular cleaning?
Dusting, kitchen countertops and exterior appliances, sinks and faucets, bathrooms, floors and trash. Inside the fridge, dishes, laundry, drawers and the oven are extras. Use the switch above to compare with an Airbnb turnover.
Do you clean offices and clinics?
Yes: offices, retail spaces, clinics, salons and small businesses, with color-coded cloths and disposable paper towels where hygiene matters most.
Are you a housekeeper?
No, and Kelly is clear about it: a house cleaner cleans and hygienizes your home based on the agreed time and services. A housekeeper manages the household on a different kind of contract.
